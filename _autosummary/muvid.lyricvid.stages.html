muvid.lyricvid.stages¶

The lyric-video production as named stages, and the settings section muvid owns.

A runner (a separate package: it composes muvid, arioso, mixing, illustration, tituli and yb, none of which muvid imports; muvid#158) needs two things from muvid and nothing else:

  • Stages — lyric_stages() returns the steps muvid owns as a {name: Stage} mapping: stem, glyphs, render, pictures and captions. A Stage is a manifest (name, what it reads, what it writes, whether it

    costs money or memory) whose run is a "module:function" string imported lazily, the same shape as muvid.subgenres.Subgenre: listing the stages imports no rendering code. Each function takes paths and primitives and returns a report dict (or the output path); they are the functions the CLI calls, with no new behaviour. Third parties and the runner find them through the entry-point group ENTRY_POINT_GROUP (muvid.lyric_stages.v1), muvid’s own included.

  • Settings — LyricProductionSettings is the part of a song spec muvid reads, with LYRIC_PRODUCTION_SCHEMA its JSON Schema. A runner mounts it as the muvid section of its spec.json and hands it to muvid through one settings argument. Timing overrides accept the bare "fine" and the recorded form {"how": "fine", "reason": "..."}.

This module is stdlib-only at import time.

muvid.lyricvid.stages.lyric_stages(*, include_plugins=True)[source]¶

The stages available, {name: Stage}, muvid’s own first and in order.

Plugins registered under ENTRY_POINT_GROUP are added after them; a plugin naming an existing stage is skipped (muvid’s own wins). A plugin that fails to load is skipped rather than taking the catalogue down.

Composite PICTURES ([{"word", "path", "credit"?}]) onto VIDEO at OUTPUT.

Each picture appears when its word is first sung and pops on each later one. block is the text block’s (x, y, w, h) the mosaic keeps clear of. Words never sung are listed under unsung and left out.
